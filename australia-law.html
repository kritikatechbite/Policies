Gambling Law in Australia

The Interactive Gambling Act 2001

The Act restricts certain online gambling services offered to people in Australia, including online casino-style games and slots. Licensed Australian operators may offer regulated wagering such as sports and racing betting, but not online pokies or casino games to Australians.

Role of ACMA

The Australian Communications and Media Authority enforces the Act. It can investigate unlicensed services, issue warnings and request that internet providers block illegal websites. It publishes information about enforcement actions.

Getting help

If gambling is affecting you or someone you know, Gambling Help Online offers free, confidential counselling 24/7 on 1800 858 858. BetStop lets you self-exclude from licensed wagering.
