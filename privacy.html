Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Who we are

ComplyWise AU is an independent educational website. We do not offer gambling, take payments or hold accounts. This policy explains how we handle personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

2. Information we collect

We collect as little as possible: technical data such as browser, device, approximate region from IP address and pages viewed; and details you choose to send if you contact us, such as name, email and message.

3. How we use it

To run and secure the site, understand which content is useful, answer enquiries, meet legal obligations and prevent misuse. We do not use information to market gambling products.

4. Cookies

We may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functions. You can block or delete them in your browser settings, though parts of the site may then work less smoothly.

5. Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with hosting and security providers under confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.

6. Overseas storage

Some providers may store data outside Australia. We take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with the APPs.

7. Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards and keep information only as long as needed, then delete or de-identify it. No system is completely secure.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. We aim to respond within 30 days.

9. Children

This site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Third-party links and fonts

We link to external organisations whose privacy practices we do not control. Our pages may load fonts from Google Fonts, which can receive your IP address.

11. Complaints

Contact us first. If unresolved, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

12. Contact

privacy@complywise-au.example

Additional provisions

13. Anonymity and pseudonymity

You can browse this website without giving us your name or any contact details. If you email us, you may use a pseudonym or choose not to identify yourself where practicable, though we may then be unable to answer certain requests, such as a request to access or correct personal information we hold about you.

14. Website logs

Like most websites, our hosting environment may record technical information when a page is requested, including your IP address, the date and time, the pages requested, the referring page and your browser and device type. We use these logs to keep the site secure, diagnose faults and understand general usage. We do not try to identify individual visitors from them unless needed to investigate misuse or comply with the law.

15. Advertising and analytics

If we use advertising or analytics tools, such as Google Ads conversion measurement or similar services, those providers may set cookies or use identifiers to measure how our pages perform. This helps us understand which information is useful. We do not use sensitive information about you for advertising, and we do not collect information about your gambling activity. You can manage personalised advertising through your Google account’s ad settings and through your browser.

16. Direct marketing

We do not send marketing emails or messages unless you have asked to receive them, and any message we do send will explain how to opt out. We never use your information to promote gambling products, and we never share your contact details with gambling operators.

17. Accuracy of information

We take reasonable steps to ensure the personal information we hold is accurate, up to date and complete. If you believe something we hold about you is wrong, tell us and we will correct it, or note your view alongside the information if we disagree that a change is needed.

18. Access and correction requests

To ask for access to your information, email the address below with enough detail for us to find it. We may ask you to verify your identity. We will respond within a reasonable period, normally 30 days, and will explain if we cannot give access, for example where the law permits or requires us to refuse. We do not charge for making a request.

19. Data breaches

If we become aware of a data breach likely to result in serious harm to individuals, we will act quickly to contain it, assess the risk and, where required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

20. Links and embedded content

Our pages link to other organisations, including regulators and support services. When you follow a link you leave our website and the privacy policy of the other organisation applies. We recommend you read it. We are not responsible for how other websites collect or use information.

21. Policy updates

We review this policy from time to time. When we make a material change we will update the date at the top of this page. Please check back occasionally. If you do not agree with a change, you should stop using the website.
