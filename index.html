Our mission

Gambling is among the most tightly regulated categories in online advertising. The rules vary by country, change often and carry real consequences, from disapproved ads to suspended accounts and legal action.

ComplyWise AU exists so that website owners, marketers and everyday readers can understand those rules clearly. We do not claim anything on behalf of any operator, we do not rank or recommend gambling services, and we do not ask you to do anything except learn.

How a policy review typically works

01

Submission

An ad, landing page or site is submitted or detected by automated systems.

02

Automated checks

Systems scan text, images, destinations and behaviour such as redirects and popups.

03

Human review

Borderline or sensitive cases, including gambling, may be reviewed by people.

04

Decision

The ad is approved, limited, or disapproved with a stated reason.

05

Appeal or fix

Advertisers correct the issue or appeal through official channels.

This is a simplified description. Each platform runs its own process; read the official policy for specifics.

Common mistakes

Hiding what a page is really about

Pages that disguise gambling content, or show reviewers something different from what users see, can be treated as deceptive. Be transparent about the topic.

Popups users cannot close

Interstitials that trap visitors, or buttons that force a click, damage user trust and commonly breach platform rules.

Implying gambling is income

Presenting gambling as a way to earn money or fix financial problems is misleading and harmful.

Ignoring local law

An ad may breach the law of the place it is shown even if the product is lawful elsewhere.

Missing responsible gambling information

Clear age limits and links to support services should be easy to find.

Australia at a glance

  • Online casino-style games and slots are restricted for people in Australia under the Interactive Gambling Act 2001.
  • ACMA enforces the law and can arrange blocking of illegal sites.
  • Licensed wagering is regulated by states and territories.
  • BetStop lets people self-exclude from licensed wagering.
  • Free, confidential help is available 24/7 on 1800 858 858.

The facts about gambling

📉

The house edge

Games are designed so the operator keeps a share of the money wagered over time.

🎲

Every round is independent

Past results never make a future result more or less likely.

💸

Not an income

Gambling is a cost and not a way to earn money or solve money problems.

⏱️

Limits and breaks

Setting limits before starting and taking breaks can reduce harm.

Who needs to know what

Advertisers should confirm licensing, any platform certification and the law of each country they target before running gambling-related ads. Ads and landing pages must match, make no misleading claims and never target minors.

Website owners should be open about what their pages are, avoid popups that cannot be closed, avoid cloaking and redirects, and show age limits and support links where gambling is discussed.

Readers should know that online casino games are restricted in Australia, that games are built with a house edge, and that free, confidential help is available 24/7 on 1800 858 858.

Go to official resources

Publisher checklist

  • Confirm the law of every country where your page or ads will appear.
  • Be clear and honest about what your page is and who it is for.
  • Show age limits (18+) and a link to free support services.
  • Never use popups that cannot be closed, or redirects that differ from the ad.
  • Avoid claims about winnings, guaranteed results or easy money.
  • Re-read the official policy whenever it is updated.

Glossary

🏷️

Certification

An approval some platforms require before gambling-related ads can run.

📜

Licence

Official authorisation from a government regulator to offer a regulated service.

🕳️

Cloaking

Showing reviewers different content from what real users see. It breaches most platform rules.

🧱

Dark pattern

A design that pushes users into actions they did not choose, such as a popup with no close option.

🧑‍⚖️

ACMA

The Australian Communications and Media Authority, which enforces the Interactive Gambling Act.

🔒

Self-exclusion

A tool letting people block themselves from licensed wagering, such as BetStop in Australia.
