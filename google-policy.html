Google’s Gambling Advertising Policy

Overview

Google Ads treats gambling and games as a restricted category. Ads are generally only permitted for specific product types, in specific countries, from advertisers who hold the relevant licences and any required Google certification. The country lists and conditions are updated regularly, so the official policy page is always the final authority.

Typical requirements

📄

Local licensing

Advertisers are generally expected to hold the authorisation required where ads run.

🏅

Certification

Many gambling advertisers need Google certification before running ads.

🎯

Targeting

Ads may only appear in approved locations and must not target minors.

🖥️

Landing pages

Pages usually need clear licensing and responsible gambling information.

Content standards

  • No guarantees of winnings or “risk-free” claims.
  • No content that appeals to minors, such as cartoon characters or youth themes.
  • No presenting gambling as a solution to financial problems.
  • Clear, accurate descriptions of what the page or product is.
  • No encouragement of excessive or irresponsible gambling.

Why ads get disapproved

  • Promoting a service not lawful in the targeted country.
  • Missing age notices or support links.
  • Redirects, cloaking or pages that differ from the ad.
  • Popups users cannot dismiss.
  • Misleading or exaggerated claims.

Official source

Read the live policy: Gambling and games – Google Ads Policy.

Policies change. Confirm details on the official page before acting.

Australia: certification status

Google runs an Australia-specific section within its Gambling and games policy. According to Google’s help notices, it paused reviewing new certification applications for online gambling providers targeting Australia on 25 August 2025, and industry reports say applications reopened from 21 April 2026. Existing rules still require advertisers to follow Australian law and any certification conditions.

Read the notice: Update to Gambling and Games Policy: Australia.

Status can change at any time. Always confirm on Google’s official page.

Questions publishers ask

Can any site run gambling ads?

No. Gambling is a restricted category with licensing and certification conditions.

Does a disclaimer make a page compliant?

No. A disclaimer helps, but the page and offer must still follow the law and policy.

Where is the current text?

On Google Ads Help, linked in the Resources page.
